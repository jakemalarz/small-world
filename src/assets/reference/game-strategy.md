# Small World: 2-Player Strategy Guide

This guide focuses on **2-player Small World strategy**, where the game becomes a "zero-sum" battle: every point you deny your opponent is as valuable as a point you gain.

## Top 10 Winning Strategies (2-Player Focus)

1. **The "Zero-Sum" Mindset**
   In 2-player games, hurting your opponent is mandatory. Unlike 4-5 player games where attacking one player lets the others get ahead, in 2-player, if you wipe out their "In Decline" tokens, you swing the score massively in your favor. Always prioritize moves that reduce their income over moves that slightly increase yours.

2. **Timing the Decline (The 2-3 Turn Rule)**
   A common novice mistake is holding onto a race too long. In 2-player, tempo is everything.
   * **Turn 1:** Expand.
   * **Turn 2:** Expand again (if profitable).
   * **Turn 3:** Usually Decline.
   * *Why?* By turn 3, your troops are spread thin. If you can't score significantly more points than your opponent (or at least 7-8 points), decline. It is often better to have a fresh, aggressive race on the board than a stagnant one earning 5-6 points.

3. **Wipe Out the "In Decline" Race**
   [cite_start]Each player can only have a single race in Decline on the map at any given time[cite: 202]. If you eliminate their In Decline tokens entirely, they earn **zero** passive points. In 2-player, you should aggressively target their gray tokens to starve their economy.

4. **The "Active Denial" Block**
   [cite_start]If your opponent has a race that relies on specific terrain (e.g., *Humans* need Farmlands [cite: 251][cite_start], *Dwarves* need Mines [cite: 267]), occupy those specific regions even if they aren't optimal for you. Denying them a +1 bonus is worth the effort in a head-to-head match.

5. **Mathematical "Checking"**
   Before your turn, calculate the "Opportunity Cost."
   * *Equation:* (Points from keeping current race) vs. (Points from declining + potential points next turn with new race).
   * If your current race will score fewer than 6-7 points, and a new race could score 8-10 next turn, Declining is mathematically superior.

6. **Use Mountains as "Savings Accounts"**
   [cite_start]When you are about to go into decline, try to position your troops on Mountains, which require an additional Race token to conquer[cite: 127]. In a 2-player game, the opponent often won't waste the resources to crack a Mountain defense for just 1 coin, ensuring you keep that passive income for multiple turns.

7. **Threaten the "Combo"**
   If a powerful combo is at the top of the stack, pay the coins to skip it if you can't take it, or take it just to deny it to your opponent. Never let your opponent get a "God Tier" combo for free.

8. **Sorcerer Counter-Play**
   [cite_start]If your opponent picks *Sorcerers*, **never** leave a single token in a region adjacent to them, as their power only works on a token that is the only race token in its Region[cite: 295]. Stack at least 2 tokens or use terrain/fortresses. In 2-player, *Sorcerers* are easily countered if you simply deny them targets.

9. **The "Stout" Tempo Swing**
   [cite_start]The **Stout** power allows you to go In Decline at the end of a regular turn of conquests, after scoring, instead of spending an entire turn going into Decline[cite: 410]. In a 2-player race, this tempo advantage is often decisive.

10. **Game End Rush**
    Track the turn marker. On the final turn, defense means nothing. Abandon all your defensive positions to spread out and grab as many territories as possible for raw points. You don't need to hold them next turn because there is no next turn.

## Race Tier List (2-Player Specific)

In 2-player games, **Aggression** and **Economy** are top-tier. Defensive races are generally weaker because a single opponent can focus all their efforts on breaking your defense.

### Tier 1: The Best (Aggressive/Swarm)
* **Amazons:** Pure aggression. [cite_start]Four of your Amazon tokens may only be used for conquest, not for defense[cite: 241]. This allows you to steamroll the opponent's active or declined race.
* **Ghouls:** The best race for tempo. [cite_start]Once In Decline, your Ghouls can continue to conquer new Regions in the following turns, playing exactly as if they were still Active tokens[cite: 278, 280].
* [cite_start]**Ratmen:** No Race benefit; their sheer number of tokens is enough[cite: 287]. Flooding the board forces the opponent to waste turns clearing you out.
* **Tritons:** On the small 2-player board, coastal regions are abundant. [cite_start]Tritons may conquer all Coastal Regions at a cost of 1 less Triton token than normal[cite: 301], which is massive for board dominance.
* [cite_start]**Giants:** Giants may conquer any Region adjacent to a Mountain Region they occupy at a cost of 1 less Giant token than normal[cite: 284]. Excellent for cracking your opponent's "Mountain Savings Accounts."

### Tier 2: Decent (Situational)
* [cite_start]**Skeletons:** You take 1 new Skeleton token from the storage tray for every 2 non-empty Regions you conquered this turn[cite: 289]. Good, but if the opponent declines early or wipes the board, Skeletons lose efficiency.
* **Humans/Wizards:** "Farming" races. [cite_start]Good if you can get the Farmland [cite: 251] [cite_start]or Magic [cite: 309] regions, but in 2-player, the opponent will actively target these lands to deny you points.
* [cite_start]**Halflings:** You place a Hole-in-the-Ground in each of the first 2 Regions you conquer, making them immune to enemy conquests[cite: 247, 248]. Annoying, but in 2-player, the opponent can just ignore your 2 invincible regions and conquer the rest of the map.

### Tier 3: Bad (Defensive/Slow)
* [cite_start]**Elves:** When the enemy conquers your Region, you keep all your Elf tokens in hand for redeployment[cite: 275]. This "Immortal" ability is weak in 2-player. You don't need to survive; you need to score. 
* [cite_start]**Dwarves:** They score 1 bonus Victory coin for Mine regions[cite: 267, 270], but they score poorly unless they sit on them, making them predictable targets.
* [cite_start]**Sorcerers:** *High Tier in 4+ players, Low Tier in 2 players.* Sorcerers can conquer a Region by substituting one of your opponent's Active tokens with one of your own[cite: 292]. A savvy opponent will simply stack 2 chips on every border, rendering your power useless.

## Special Power Tier List (2-Player Specific)

### Tier 1: Game Breakers
* [cite_start]**Spirit:** When the Race tokens associated with your Spirit Special Power go into Decline, they never count toward the limit regarding having a single In Decline race on the map[cite: 403]. Having two In-Decline races generating points is devastating in a 1v1 economy.
* [cite_start]**Commando / Berserk:** Commando lets you conquer any Region with 1 less Race token than normal [cite: 345][cite_start], and Berserk lets you use the Reinforcement die before each of your conquests[cite: 321]. This efficiency lets you wipe out the opponent's decline race and still grab land.
* [cite_start]**Pillaging:** Each non-empty Region you conquer this turn is worth 1 bonus Victory coin at turn's end[cite: 396]. Directly rewards the "Zero-Sum" strategy of attacking the opponent.

### Tier 2: Solid Picks
* [cite_start]**Merchant:** Collect 1 bonus Victory coin for any Region you occupy at turn's end[cite: 390]. Great for simple scoring, provided you have enough race tokens to hold land.
* [cite_start]**Flying:** You may conquer any Region of the map except Seas and Lakes, and they do not need to be adjacent to ones you already occupy[cite: 364, 365]. Prevents the opponent from "blocking" you.
* [cite_start]**Stout:** As mentioned, the tempo gain of going In Decline at the end of a regular turn [cite: 410] is excellent.

### Tier 3: Traps / Weak
* [cite_start]**Diplomat:** **Be Careful.** You may select one opponent whose Active race you did not attack this turn as your ally, and they cannot attack your active race until your next turn[cite: 347, 348]. In 2-player, it basically skips the combat phase. If you are behind, this power is useless.
* [cite_start]**Wealthy:** Collect 7 bonus Victory coins, once only, at the end of your first turn[cite: 429]. 7 coins is nice, but a power that helps you every turn will usually net more points over the life of a race.

## Best Combinations (The "God Tier")
If you see these, take them immediately.

1. **Spirit Ghouls:** You can keep attacking with your Ghouls even when they are your *second* decline race. This is an engine of destruction.
2. **Commando Amazons:** You can virtually wipe the entire board in a single turn.
3. **Flying Sorcerers:** Mitigates the 2-player weakness of Sorcerers by letting you target *any* lone token on the map, not just adjacent ones.
4. **Merchant Skeletons:** You get points for conquering and extra points for holding.

## Decline & Placement Strategies

**When to Decline:**
* **The "Rule of 4":** If you cannot conquer at least **2** new regions on your turn, or if your expected score is less than **4-5** points, decline.
* **The Turn Before:** If you plan to decline next turn, attack aggressively this turn. Spread out to thin your troops so you can redeploy them effectively when you decline.

**Board Placement (Tactical Movement):**
* **The "Shell" Defense:** When redeploying active troops, place your strongest stack on the border facing the opponent, and your weaker stacks behind them.
* [cite_start]**Abandoning Regions:** If a player wishes to free up some more Race tokens, he may opt to entirely empty up some or all Regions, leaving no Tokens there[cite: 193]. Redeploying allows you to attack from a new angle or abandon a flank that is too costly to defend.
* **Blocking Chokepoints:** Overstack choke points to force the opponent to use "Flying" or go the long way around.
